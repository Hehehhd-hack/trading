Risk Rules

Non-negotiable. Tape these to your monitor.

01

1% Rule

Never risk more than 1% of account on a single trade. 3 losses in a row = still alive.

02

Define the Stop First

If you can't name the invalidation price before entry, you don't have a trade — you have a hope.

03

Daily Max Loss

Hit 3% down on the day? Close the platform. Tomorrow exists. Today's tilt does not get funded.

04

Two Setups, Max

Master two patterns. One is a scalpel, one is a hammer. Everything else is noise.

05

Journal Every Trade

Screenshot, thesis, outcome, emotion. Data beats memory every single time.

06

Size Down When Wrong

Losing streak = reduce size, not increase it. Revenge sizing is how accounts die.
